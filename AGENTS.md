# Commit Messages

"feat: [message]" followed by an empty line, followed by 15-30 sentences in this format: "- [tag]: [message]" where `[message]` is a string less than or equal to 40 chars with no period (other punctuation is fine if needed) at the end, and `[tag]` identifies work like "ui" or "app" or "platform" -- do not use sentence case (prefer lowercase) except for proper nouns, initialisms, or acronymns.
